Polly · animations

Polly, moving.

One idle loop per mood. Calm moods move slowly, alarming moods move fast, so the rhythm alone tells you how bad it is. Motion stops when the system asks for reduced motion.

OK · idle on the perch
Slow breathing, a look around every few seconds, a blink. 4–6 s loops.
“Polly is quiet. Go back to sleep.”
Warning · squawking
Beak snaps open and shut, crest flaps, body fidgets on the perch. 0.6 s squawk, 1.2 s fidget.
“Polly is fidgeting: 3 warnings.”
Critical · on its back
Feet kick out of sync, body rocks, dizzy stars circle the head. Fast and a little ridiculous.
“Polly is squawking: 1 host down.”
Unreachable · confused
Head tilts side to side, listening. Heavy eyelid, a question mark that bobs. Slow and puzzled.
“Polly can't reach monitor.webwerf.nl.”
